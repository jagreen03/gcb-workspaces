# Objective

Evaluate the hardware area, latency, and optical-bandwidth overhead of the
`TKT-019.8` integrity mitigations (Epoch Tagging + discard-on-mismatch pop;
Sarwate-table CRC32; demux reassembly watchdog) **purely as a physical-cost
question, assuming [[TKT-019.8]]'s Findings A and B are eventually
resolved**. This ticket does not resolve, minimize, or touch those findings
— it answers a different question than `TKT-019.8` asked: *given the
mitigation mechanism is correct (a premise this ticket does not verify),
what does it cost?* A cost evaluation is not a correctness re-validation,
and nothing here should be read as narrowing or softening either open
defect.

## Method

Baseline = the last git-committed RTL (`HEAD`, commit `6f40636`) for
`gcb_dwdm_deskew_cdc.sv`, `gcb_dwdm_payload_mux.sv`, `gcb_dwdm_payload_demux.sv`
— i.e. pre-`TKT-019.8`, no epoch tag, no CRC32, no watchdog. Mitigated = the
current uncommitted working-tree RTL (`TKT-019.8`'s changes). Both extracted
read-only (`git show`/direct read — the working tree was not staged,
committed, or discarded) and run through the identical `sv2v` → Yosys 0.68
`read_verilog; chparam; hierarchy -check; proc; opt_clean; check -assert;
select -assert-count 0 t:$dlatch t:$dlatchsr t:$sr; stat` pipeline already
established in `sim/yosys068_bless/`, at both the cocotb bring-up geometry
and the `NUM_COLORS=60` GCB-034 Mode B ceiling. This is a **digital cell-count
and elaboration-time comparison only** — no synthesis, no timing closure, no
physical PPA numbers. See the Doctrine (`gcb-wiki/14`) §1.2 for why that
distinction matters and is preserved here too.

## Out of scope

1. Any RTL change to close Findings A or B.
2. Real synthesis/PPA (standard-cell area, routed timing, power) — cell
   *counts* from Yosys `stat` are a structural proxy, not a physical
   measurement.
3. Re-validating that the mitigation is functionally correct — that is
   `TKT-019.8`'s open question, not this ticket's.

## Execution Log

### A. `gcb_dwdm_deskew_cdc` — Epoch Isolation only

| Geometry | Baseline cells (total, incl. submodules) | Mitigated cells | Δ | Δ% | Elaboration time (base → mitig.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bring-up (`NUM_COLORS=4`) | 188 | 238 | **+50** | +26.6% | 0.14s → 0.04s |
| Mode B ceiling (`NUM_COLORS=60`) | 2844 | 3510 | **+666** | +23.4% | 0.25s → 0.33s |

Per-lane `gcb_dwdm_async_skew_fifo` cell count is **unchanged** by epoch
tagging at either geometry (39 cells bring-up, 45 cells at ceiling — that
45-vs-39 difference is `MAX_SKEW_CYCLES`-driven gray-code pointer width,
present identically in baseline; not an epoch effect). Epoch tagging's real
storage cost is in **memory bits**, not logic cells: `+64 bits` at bring-up
(4 lanes × 4 extra bits × `DEPTH=16`), `+30,720 bits` at the ceiling (60 × 4
× `DEPTH=128`) — pure register-file widening, zero added comparators/muxes
per bit. The `+50`/`+666` cell delta is **entirely** the top-level
`epoch_match`/`lane_ready`/`epoch_discard` combinational compare-and-discard
logic, and scales linearly with `NUM_COLORS` (≈12.5 cells/lane at bring-up,
≈11.1 cells/lane at the ceiling) — one small comparator-class circuit per
lane, no new pipeline stage, no submodule count change (still exactly
`NUM_COLORS` FIFO instances either way).

**The 60-lane buffering itself, not the mitigation, is the dominant cost of
this module.** At the ceiling, the 60 async-FIFO instances alone total 2700
cells (`60 × 45`) — **94.9% of the 2844-cell baseline** and still 76.9% of
the 3510-cell mitigated total. Epoch isolation adds a real but secondary
23-27% on top of a buffering cost that was already there and is unrelated
to `TKT-019.8`.

### B. `gcb_dwdm_payload_mux` — CRC32 (+ the `tx_epoch` output register)

| Geometry | Baseline cells | Mitigated cells | Δ | Δ% / multiplier | Elaboration time (base → mitig.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bring-up (`NUM_COLORS=8`) | 8,355 | 42,923 | **+34,568** | +413.8% (5.14×) | 99s → 265s |
| Mode B ceiling (`NUM_COLORS=60`) | 13,895 | 59,215 | **+45,320** | +326.2% (4.26×) | 275s → 558s |

### C. `gcb_dwdm_payload_demux` — CRC32 (+ the reassembly watchdog)

| Geometry | Baseline cells | Mitigated cells | Δ | Δ% / multiplier | Elaboration time (base → mitig.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bring-up (`NUM_COLORS=8`) | 3,377 | 13,752 | **+10,375** | +307.2% (4.07×) | 15.9s → 36s |
| Mode B ceiling (`NUM_COLORS=60`) | 5,197 | 15,572 | **+10,375** | +199.6% (3.00×) | 380.3s → 491s |

The demux's absolute CRC delta is **identical** (+10,375 cells) at both
geometries — it scales with `MAX_PAYLOAD_BYTES` (the CRC loop bound), not
`NUM_COLORS`; the lower *percentage* at the ceiling is baseline's own
lane-accumulation logic growing, diluting the same fixed CRC cost, not CRC
getting cheaper.

**CRC32 is the area driver in both modules, overwhelmingly.** The
`tx_epoch` output register (mux) and the watchdog counter+comparator
(demux) each cost on the order of a few dozen cells — essentially the
entire four- to five-figure delta above is the CRC32 logic. Mechanism: this
CRC is computed **combinationally, in one cycle, at `start`** — a fully
unrolled loop over up to 1,027 header+payload bytes, each step indexing the
256-entry constant `CRC32_TABLE` (itself a 256-way constant select — the
dominant contributor to the `$mux` count jump, e.g. mux bring-up
`$mux: 7,192 → 20,901`). A fully-unrolled single-cycle CRC is exactly
`N` byte-update circuits in silicon, not `N` cycles through one reused
circuit — the classic combinational-vs-iterative area/latency trade. **A
future ticket could cut this 4-5× multiplier substantially by pipelining
the CRC update over multiple cycles through one shared table lookup
instead of unrolling it** — not evaluated further here (a design decision
for a later ticket, not assumed or recommended as necessary).

## B. Area / Latency / Bandwidth summary (answers Task 4's question directly)

| Mechanism | Area | Latency | Optical bandwidth |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Epoch isolation** | Small, linear in `NUM_COLORS` (~11-13 cells/lane); secondary to the pre-existing 60-lane FIFO buffering cost | **Zero** on the clean path (pure sideband tag, no new pipeline stage). Non-zero only on the recovery path: each stale-epoch word costs one extra drain cycle before realignment — this is exactly the path Finding B's idle-gap scenario would make worse, not a new latency concern on its own | **Zero** — the epoch tag never leaves the CDC; it is not part of the framed byte stream, never serialized onto the optical channel |
| **CRC32 (Sarwate table)** | **Large** — 4-5× the pre-mitigation mux/demux cell count, driven by the fully-unrolled single-cycle table-lookup construction (see above) | **+0 to +1 EMIT cycle** per transaction (the 4-byte trailer can push the padded word count up by one `CHUNK_BYTES`-sized word); the CRC computation itself is single-cycle/combinational and adds no separate cycles | **+4 bytes per transaction, fixed**, transmitted in-band. ≈0.39% of a 1024 B Mode B payload; ≈1.48% of a ≈271 B Mode A payload — a real, if small, tax, and proportionally worse for Mode A |
| **Reassembly watchdog** | Negligible (one counter + one comparator) | **Zero** on the clean path. Bounds (does not add) worst-case recovery latency to `REASSEMBLY_TIMEOUT` cycles, replacing an undetected indefinite hang — a latency *improvement* on the failure path this ticket is not re-litigating | **Zero** — purely a receiver-local timer, nothing transmitted |

**Bottom line for the Architect: epoch isolation and the watchdog are cheap
by any measure tested. CRC32 is not** — it is the area cost center of
`TKT-019.8` by a wide margin (4-5×, tens of thousands of cells at the
60-lane ceiling), though the 60-lane skew-FIFO buffering itself remains the
single largest line item in the CDC stage specifically, independent of any
mitigation. None of this changes Findings A or B's status.
