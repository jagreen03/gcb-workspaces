# Objective

`TKT-019.10` proved Candidate 2 (the `CDC_IDLE`/`CDC_ACTIVE` watchdog-arming
gate in `gcb_dwdm_deskew_cdc`) closed under a specific, narrow set of
conditions: a shared, zero-skew clock, a single withheld lane, and no epoch
wraparound. Every one of those simplifications was a physical assumption the
digital testbench abstracted away, not a property proven to hold. This
ticket's job is to attack exactly those assumptions — independent per-lane
clock skew (the CDC's own stated *raison d'être*, per `TKT-019.3`), multiple
simultaneous stale lanes/words, mixed stale/current/future epoch
populations, and epoch rollover (`EPOCH_W=4`'s mod-16 aliasing) — as
falsification attempts, not confirmation runs. A test that merely re-passes
under a condition already proven is not this ticket's job; a test that finds
nothing wrong under a condition never before exercised is only worth as much
as the adversarial effort actually spent trying to break it, and that effort
must be documented, not assumed adequate after the fact.

`gcb-wiki/14`'s epistemic labels apply throughout: every result here is
`PROVEN`/`FAILED`/`OPEN DEFECT` — DIGITAL SIMULATION, TESTED CONDITIONS
ONLY, never a physical, silicon, or BER claim. This ticket does not
implement a fix for anything it finds; per this epic's standing discipline,
a finding is reported and held for disposition, not silently patched.

## Scope

1. **Independent per-lane clock skew, genuinely stressed** — randomized
   phase, deterministic frequency offset per lane, skew approaching and
   exceeding the async skew FIFO's own `DEPTH` (`MAX_SKEW_CYCLES`), and
   worst-case (reverse/randomized) lane arrival ordering. Verify the
   `CDC_IDLE`/`CDC_ACTIVE` gate and the bounded-wait/watchdog semantics
   survive all of it — not just that a clean transaction still completes.
2. **Multi-lane, multi-word stale/future epoch attacks** — multiple lanes
   simultaneously carrying multiple queued stale words each, and a
   deliberately-injected *future*-epoch word (epoch tag ahead of
   `expected_epoch`, not behind it) on top of a genuine mixed population.
   Verify the stated invariant: no word whose epoch differs from
   `expected_epoch` may activate the watchdog, satisfy alignment, or
   contaminate the transaction being assembled.
3. **Epoch rollover** — force `expected_epoch` through a full `EPOCH_W=4`
   (mod-16) wrap, then construct the specific adversarial case the finite
   width structurally cannot distinguish from legitimate current data: a
   word whose epoch tag numerically *aliases* `expected_epoch` after a full
   wrap, even though it did not originate in the current epoch window. This
   is not assumed safe or unsafe in advance — it is run and reported either
   way.
4. **Physical-cost re-evaluation** — `TKT-019.9` costed the CDC before
   Candidate 2 existed. Re-run the identical Yosys 0.68 bless methodology
   against the current `gcb_dwdm_deskew_cdc.sv` and report the actual
   incremental cost of the `CDC_IDLE`/`CDC_ACTIVE` FSM plus
   `any_matched_activity`, at both the cocotb bring-up and `NUM_COLORS=60`
   geometries — not an estimate of what it "should" cost.

## Out of scope

1. Any RTL fix for a finding this ticket surfaces — held for disposition,
   per standing discipline, exactly as `TKT-019.8`'s Findings A/B were.
2. Real (physical) clock skew, jitter, or any silicon/PIC-receiver
   measurement — "independent clock" here means genuinely distinct cocotb
   `Clock` sources at different, and where stated randomized, periods and
   phases in digital simulation, nothing more.
3. Re-litigating Finding A (length-count) or the mux-side idle-gap test —
   both already closed in `TKT-019.10`; this ticket is specifically about
   conditions `TKT-019.10` itself listed as still unexercised.

## Required proof

For each of Scope items 1-3: a test that was actually run, with the
specific stimulus and observed RTL response reported — pass or fail — not
a claim that the mechanism "should" handle it. For item 4: actual Yosys
0.68 `stat` output at both geometries, diffed cell-by-cell against
`TKT-019.9`'s recorded baseline numbers for `gcb_dwdm_deskew_cdc`.

### A testbench-side finding, surfaced and fixed before any of the above
could be trusted (falsification discipline applied to this ticket's OWN
instrumentation, not just the RTL): the first attempt at all four new
tests failed or produced symptoms (`fifo_full` tripping at push #11
instead of #16; `expected_epoch` not advancing; `txn_void` never firing)
that looked exactly like a real RTL defect. Root-caused, in order:

1. A orphaned-`Clock`-task cross-test contamination (cocotb does not kill
   a `cocotb.start_soon` task just because the test that started it
   returns) — fixed by capturing every clock-driver task handle and
   calling `.kill()` on it in a `finally` block in every test that starts
   colour/rd clocks.
2. The actual, dominant cause: `push_color`'s `in_valid` strobe used a
   read-modify-write idiom — `dut.in_valid.value = _i(dut.in_valid) |
   (1 << c)` to set, the mirror to clear. Reading `dut.in_valid.value`
   immediately after writing it, with no intervening `await`, returned
   the PRE-write value in this cocotb 1.9.2 / Verilator 5.032
   combination — so every "clear" silently lost every earlier lane's
   "set," and `in_valid` latched high (observed stuck at `0b111` across
   three lanes). Since `wr_en = in_valid[c] & channel_enable[c]`, a
   stuck-high `in_valid[c]` means **every subsequent `wr_clk` edge on
   that lane silently writes another duplicate word**, filling the FIFO
   far faster than the explicit push count implied — exactly reproducing
   the "`fifo_full` at push #11, not #16" symptom. Confirmed by direct
   instrumentation (logging `pre`/`post_set`/`post_clear` per push)
   before concluding this was a testbench hazard, not an RTL defect.
   Fixed in all five `push_color` closures by driving `in_valid` from a
   pure-Python shadow variable (`in_valid_val`), exactly the pattern
   `flat_val`/`epoch_flat_val` already used for the other two signals —
   never reading the signal back to compute the next write.
3. Two explicit-timing-margin issues (round 101's delayed-lane arrival;
   the multi-lane/rollover tests' `ALIGN_TIMEOUT + 2` poll windows not
   accounting for cross-domain sync latency on top of the wait-counter
   itself) — fixed by widening margins to `ALIGN_TIMEOUT + 6`/`+ 10` and,
   for round 101 specifically, explicitly tracking `saw_void` rather than
   inferring it from a side effect.

None of this is an RTL finding. It is recorded here because this epic's
own discipline ("do not assume a fix is correct before proving it")
applies equally to test infrastructure — a false failure chased as if it
were a real one, or worse, a false PASS from a testbench that silently
wasn't exercising what it claimed to, would have been exactly the kind of
unproven claim this project exists to prevent.

### Task 2 — Clock Skew & CDC Stress: **PROVEN — DIGITAL SIMULATION,
TESTED CONDITIONS.** `test_deskew_cdc_randomized_skew_and_worst_case_arrival`
ran 4 independently-clocked lanes with per-lane clock START TIME
randomized (`rng.uniform(0,3)` ns phase offset, on top of the pre-existing
4 incommensurate `WR_PERIODS`), 6 rounds of randomized arrival order +
randomized delay, plus 3 explicit worst-case rounds: full-reverse order,
last lane delayed to within 6 cycles of `ALIGN_TIMEOUT` (26 of 32), and
last lane delayed to `MAX_SKEW_CYCLES - 1` (15 of 16). Every round
reconstructed byte-exact with zero spurious `txn_void` and correct
`expected_epoch` advancement. `test_deskew_cdc_skew_exceeds_fifo_capacity`
drove 20 words into a 16-slot FIFO on 3 lanes while the 4th was withheld:
each lane accepted exactly `DEPTH=16` words then silently dropped the
remaining 4 (no corruption of retained data); `stuck` named only the
genuinely-empty lane, never the overflowing ones (the documented
`stuck = channel_enable & fifo_empty` asymmetry — it cannot name an
overflowing lane, only an empty one — confirmed empirically, not merely
read off the RTL); recovery after the stale backlog drained was
byte-exact. The `CDC_IDLE`/`CDC_ACTIVE` gate and bounded-wait/watchdog
semantics survived all of it.

### Task 3 — Multi-Lane Stale & Rollover Attacks:
`test_deskew_cdc_multi_lane_stale_and_future_epoch` forced lanes 0 and 1
to each carry TWO queued stale words from two different past epochs,
injected a deliberately wrong-direction FUTURE-epoch word
(`expected_epoch + 2`) on lane 2, and confirmed: the future-epoch word was
observed being discarded via `epoch_discard` (never treated as ready,
`aligned_valid` never fired on it alone); the real transaction, once
supplied, reconstructed byte-exact on all 4 lanes with zero contamination
from either stale backlog; no third spurious `txn_void`. **PROVEN —
DIGITAL SIMULATION, TESTED CONDITIONS**, for this specific mixed
population.

`test_deskew_cdc_epoch_rollover_aliasing` drove `expected_epoch` through
an exact `EPOCH_W=4` (mod-16) wrap via 16 consecutive forced `txn_void`
events, confirmed it landed back on exactly 0, then constructed the
aliasing case: a "straggler" word tagged epoch=0 on lane 3, delivered
alongside the genuine real epoch-0 transaction's correct words on lanes
0-2. Result: **`aligned_valid` fired, and lane 3 reconstructed as the
straggler's value, not the real one.**

**FINDING (OPEN, not fixed by this ticket, per standing discipline): epoch
rollover aliasing is real under tested conditions.** A word tagged with an
epoch value that numerically coincides with `expected_epoch` after a full
`EPOCH_W=4` wrap is indistinguishable from — and is accepted as —
legitimate current-epoch data, regardless of when it actually originated.
This is a **structural property of a finite-width epoch counter** (the CDC
carries no generation/sequence counter beyond the 4-bit tag itself), not
an implementation defect in Candidate 2 specifically — Candidate 2's own
invariant ("no word whose epoch != expected_epoch may activate, satisfy,
or contaminate") held exactly as designed; the aliasing straggler's epoch
DID equal `expected_epoch` by construction, so epoch_match was correctly
1, not a misfire of the comparator. Whether this is a practical risk
depends entirely on whether a real stale word can physically survive 16
full epoch generations in flight, which this ticket does not and cannot
evaluate (see Out of scope #2) — reported and held for disposition, not
silently patched, exactly as `TKT-019.8`'s Findings A/B were.

identical `sv2v` → `read_verilog; chparam; hierarchy -check; proc;
opt_clean; check -assert; select -assert-count 0 t:$dlatch t:$dlatchsr
t:$sr; stat` pipeline as `TKT-019.9`, against the current (Candidate-2-
inclusive) `gcb_dwdm_deskew_cdc.sv`. `check -assert` and the latch-freedom
assertion both passed (exit 0) at both geometries.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bring-up (`NUM_COLORS=4`) | 238 | **248** | **+10** | +4.2% |
| Mode B ceiling (`NUM_COLORS=60`) | 3510 | **3520** | **+10** | +0.28% |

**The delta is identical (+10 cells) at both geometries** — unlike epoch
isolation's own TKT-019.9 cost (+50/+666, scaling ~linearly with
`NUM_COLORS`), Candidate 2's FSM cost is **fixed, O(1), independent of lane
count**. Cell-mix diff at the ceiling (local, excluding submodules): `$adff`
4→5 (+1, the one-bit `CDC_IDLE`/`CDC_ACTIVE` state register), `$mux` 73→79

`$reduce_or`/`$reduce_and` both **unchanged** (2 and 1 respectively at

each multi-bit RTLIL cell once regardless of its operand width; a
`NUM_COLORS`-wide bitwise-AND-then-OR-reduce is one `$and` cell feeding the
same reduction machinery the barrier's own `aligned_valid` already needed,
not a second per-lane-replicated structure. No per-lane FIFO submodule
cost changed (still exactly `39`/`45` cells each, identical to `TKT-019.9`).
This is a digital cell-count comparison only — no synthesis, no timing
closure, no physical PPA number (`gcb-wiki/14` §1.2).

## Telemetry & Evaluation

**Scope 1 (clock skew/CDC stress):** PROVEN — DIGITAL SIMULATION, TESTED
CONDITIONS. The `CDC_IDLE`/`CDC_ACTIVE` gate and bounded-wait semantics
held under randomized phase, deterministic frequency offset, skew at and
past the FIFO's own `DEPTH`, and worst-case arrival ordering — conditions
`TKT-019.10` itself had not exercised. Not a claim about real PIC-receiver
clock recovery, jitter, or silicon CDC behavior.

**Scope 2 (multi-lane stale/future-epoch):** PROVEN — DIGITAL SIMULATION,
TESTED CONDITIONS, for the specific mixed population constructed (two
two-deep stale backlogs + one future-epoch word + a forced void). Not an
exhaustive sweep of every possible population shape.

**Scope 3 (epoch rollover):** **FAILED to remain closed** under the
specific aliasing construction tested — but correctly classified as an
**OPEN DEFECT that is a structural property of the finite-width epoch
counter**, not a Candidate 2 implementation bug (Candidate 2's own stated
invariant held; the failure is one level up, in what "matching epoch"
can mean after 16 full wraps with no generation counter). Held for
disposition, not patched here.

**Scope 4 (physical cost):** Candidate 2 costs a fixed **+10 cells** at
both tested geometries — cheap by the same standard `TKT-019.9` applied to
epoch isolation and the watchdog, and far cheaper than CRC32's 4-5×
multiplier. Digital cell-count only, not a physical PPA claim.

No PBG grade assigned to this ticket as a whole — it mixes a closed
falsification result (Scope 1/2/4) with a genuinely new open finding
(Scope 3); grading the bundle would obscure that the rollover finding is
unresolved. `TKT-019.8` Findings A and B are unaffected and remain
`PROVEN — DIGITAL SIMULATION, TESTED CONDITIONS` per `TKT-019.10`; this
ticket neither reopens nor extends that closure — it found a *new*,
narrower, previously-undocumented boundary (epoch aliasing) that `gcb-
wiki/14` should now list explicitly rather than under a general "epoch
rollover: untested" caveat.
